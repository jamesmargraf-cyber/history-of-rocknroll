What is harmony, and why does it matter?

A melody is a single line of notes moving through time. Harmony is what happens beneath it — the chords that give that melody color, tension, and emotional direction. Most of what you feel when a song seems "happy" or "sad," "resolved" or "unresolved," comes not from the melody alone but from the harmony underneath it.

A chord progression is a sequence of chords that repeats throughout a song or a section of it. Progressions are among the most powerful tools a songwriter has. Certain progressions create such a recognizable emotional effect that they appear across hundreds — sometimes thousands — of songs, spanning decades and genres. Once you can hear them, you'll start noticing them everywhere.
